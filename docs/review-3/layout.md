## Blocks checked, against the reference, with margin

**JLC placement (CPL).** `cpl_pin1.py` is independent of hwde's `cpl_verify.py`. It fits the LCSC EasyEDA model pads, turned by the CPL rotation, onto same-numbered board pads.
- Results: Lite 145/145, Plus 293/293, Ultra 292/293 (U19: no model, THT), front 36/36. Worst pad error 0.135 mm (Lite U6 SOP-16).
- hwde `cpl_verify` agrees where it was not rate-limited (Lite 145/145, Plus 293/293).
- Pin-name check: no vendor GND pin lands on a non-GND net. The flagged pins are intentional straps: MODSEL/AMx (U8), ADDR, HEAD, V-, PCM5102A/ES7243E config pins.
- All parts are top-side and there are no bottom placements. Margin: 0 failures.

**JLC stock (`jlc_stock.py`, JLC API, 2026-10-30).**
- Lite: 53 lines, 19 extended, all in stock (tightest: L1-L4 C48391614, 1676 against 21 needed).
- Plus/Ultra: 82/80 lines, 39 extended. One fails: TPA3220 0 (R3-LAYOUT-4). Thinnest otherwise: ES9018K2M 705, Y1 49.152 MHz 497, PLR237/T8BK 236.
- Front: 18 lines, 6 extended, all in stock.

**DRC.** kicad-cli 10.0.6 with `--refill-zones --schematic-parity --severity-all`: 0/0/0 on all three boards.

**Footprint vs library (`fp_vs_lib.py`).** Lite 30/30 and front 36/36 match. On core, J4/J5 mismatch (R3-LAYOUT-12).

**Thermal (`thermal_calc.py`).**
- Lite TPA3118, theta 22 (TI 2-layer EVM, SLOS708G 6.4) to 30 C/W:
  - 1/8 clip, 6 ohm, 52 C box: 1.07 W, Tj 76-84 C. Margin 66 K to the 150 C OTE.
  - Governed limit: see R3-LAYOUT-13.
- core TPA3220, theta 30 C/W (23.5 JEDEC 2s2p x1.1 for 0.5 oz inner x1.15 for the second chip):
  - Governed limit: ≤1.05 W, Tj 93 C. Margin 22 K to OTW min 115 C (SLASEN3B 7.5). The 4-layer In1 GND plane covers 93 % of the board.
- core Q1 AO4485: F.Cu PVDD patch plus 12 vias into the 2833 mm² In2 PVDD pour (round-1 THERMAL-7 fix holds).

**Buck hot loops.** See R3-LAYOUT-7 and R3-LAYOUT-15.
- Lite SY8089: C11 100 nF at 1.7/2.6 mm and C10 10 uF at 3.5/4.4 mm from IN/GND. Within the datasheet's "close to IN and GND".
- core SY8089 U5: C15 10 uF at 5.2/3.7 mm. Acceptable at 5 V in.
- SW nodes are short on all bucks.

**Amp decoupling and bootstrap.** Not redone here; see R3-AMP-1/2 (core) in amp.md. On Lite, the amp inputs are 4-12 mm long and 100 % over GND (`gnd_ref.py`).

**Ground strategy.**
- Lite (2-layer): B.Cu GND covers 82 % and F.Cu 68 %. The amp/power section (right) and the codec/ESP32 (left) are separated by B.Cu track channels and join near J1 (top) and at the bottom, so class-D return current does not cross under the ES8388. The amp inputs are differential.
- core: In1 is a solid GND plane (93 %). F.Cu signals are well referenced. B.Cu signals reference the split In2 (R3-LAYOUT-2).

**ESD (`esd_scan.py`).**
- VBUS TVS ≤5.6 mm from the jack on both boards. core D± has a USBLC6.
- RCA inputs have 15k (Lite) and 4.7k (core) series resistors. Speakers go through the LC filter. Ethernet goes through HR911105A magnetics.
- Gaps: R3-LAYOUT-14.

**Antenna.** Keep-out clean on both boards. See R3-LAYOUT-9.

**Enclosure.** `enclosure/core.board.json` holes are derived from the board (H3 116.45/3.55 = board 151.04/217.15). They match.

## Field issues searched and what our boards do

- **JLC CPL rotation flips (SOT-23/SOIC):** immune. EasyEDA-imported footprints; 0/766 failures.
- **PowerPAD/QFN voiding with open vias under the paste:** has it (minor, R3-LAYOUT-8/16). The thermal margin absorbs it.
- **Class-D noise and EMI from long inputs near the power stage (TI SLASEN3B 11.1):** core has it (R3-LAYOUT-1). Lite is immune.
- **TPA3118 thermal shutdown on 2-layer boards at sustained power:** Lite is marginal only at the 4 ohm governed limit (R3-LAYOUT-13).
- **ESP32 antenna detuned by copper beside it:** mild on both boards (R3-LAYOUT-9).
- **Library fix not pushed to the board:** core has it (R3-LAYOUT-12).
- Known unknown: the forum and GitHub search (E2E, Louder-ESP32, squeezelite-esp32 boards) was not completed this round. The issues above come from vendor documents and our own measurements.
