HiFi Lite firmware: the makers' build

Flash a HiFi Lite (ESP32-WROVER-E, 8 MB flash) from this page with Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Plug the board into this computer over USB, then press the button and pick its serial port.

This browser can't reach a serial port. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. The page has to be opened over https (or from localhost) to use the serial port.

A first install offers to erase the board: say yes, so no old settings are left behind. The whole flash is about 5 MB and takes a minute or two.

What you get

  • AirPlay: the speaker shows up as hifi-lite- and a few letters and digits of its own (from its hardware address, such as hifi-lite-8a0cbe) on an iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • Bluetooth: pair a phone with the same hifi-lite-… name.
  • Lyrion Music Server (LMS): the board is a Squeezebox player on your network.
  • The app: open http://hifi-lite.local (or the board's IP address) in a phone's browser for volume, sources, sound settings and the other speakers on your Wi-Fi. Add it to the home screen to open it like an app.
  • Updates by USB: come back to this page for a newer build.

What's missing

  • Spotify Connect. It needs a Spotify developer key built into the firmware, and that key can't be shared, so this build leaves Spotify out. You can build your own with your own key (how to rebuild).
  • Updates over Wi-Fi and the lab's remote tools. They need the lab's keys, which only the lab's own boards carry.

First start

  1. After the flash the board restarts and, with no Wi-Fi saved, opens its own network: hifi-lite-… (the same name as above), password squeezelite.
  2. Join it from a phone or laptop and open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password. The board joins it. Its own network may stay up for a while; you can ignore it.
  4. Back on your home Wi-Fi, open http://hifi-lite.local.
